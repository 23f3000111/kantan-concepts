What we found

A good business behind a slow site

Measured on 8 October 2026 with Google Lighthouse and a page-by-page review. Speed scores change a little between runs.

32–36

Mobile speed score

Out of 100 on Google’s own test. The main content takes about 5 seconds to appear on a phone.

~5 MB

Homepage weight

200 files and 70 scripts load before a visitor sees a single paste.

3

Buttons over the phone number

On mobile, the cart, WhatsApp and reCAPTCHA buttons cover your phone number and the “Fresh ingredients” line on the very first screen.

2

Broken files on every page

Two theme files return errors each time a page loads.

75/176

Images without descriptions

Google and screen readers can’t tell what these homepage images show.

0

Recipes Google can feature

Your 10 video recipes are marked up as articles, so they can’t appear as recipe results with photos and cooking times.

4

Messages in one slider

The homepage rotates shopper offers and OEM pitches, so neither audience gets a clear message in the first five seconds.

6.7

WordPress version

At least two major versions behind, along with an older page builder and slider plugin.

1

Manual payment step

Bank-transfer customers have to email a screenshot of their receipt before the order ships.
